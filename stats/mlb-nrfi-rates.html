How often is there no run in the first inning? In the 2026 MLB regular season, 49.4% of 2,429 games stayed scoreless through the first (NRFI). The Marlins led the league at 60% of their games; the Rockies were last at 38%. The tables below come from the official inning-by-inning line scores and cover the regular season through September 27, 2026. Our NRFI and YRFI picks start from a 20,000-simulation model of each game rather than from these rates, but the rates are the context every bettor should know.

What percentage of MLB games are NRFI?

In the 2026 regular season, 49.4% of games had no run in the first inning. The rate moves a few points from season to season and with the weather, and it varies a lot by starting pitcher.

Which team has the best NRFI record in 2026?

The Miami Marlins, with no first-inning run in 97 of 162 games (60%). The Colorado Rockies had the lowest rate at 38%.

Which starting pitcher has the best first-inning record?

Andrew Alvarez, who allowed no first-inning run in 16 of 17 starts (94%) in 2026. The list above shows every starter with 15 or more starts.

Does a high team NRFI rate make NRFI a good bet?

Only if the price does not already reflect it. Books price NRFI from the same starters, lineups and parks, so a team’s record is the starting point, not the edge. Our model simulates every game 20,000 times with that day’s starters, lineups and weather and compares the result to the posted price; see MLB NRFI and YRFI picks and the NRFI guide.
