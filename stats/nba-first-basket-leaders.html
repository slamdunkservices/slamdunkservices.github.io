Who scored the most first baskets in the NBA in 2025-26?

Victor Wembanyama, with 20 first baskets of the game in 77 starts (26%). Jalen Brunson was second with 19.

How often does the home team score first in the NBA?

In 2025-26, the home team made the first field goal in 52% of games.

How often does the team that wins the tipoff score first?

In 833 of 1,315 games with a recorded opening tip (63%), the team that won it also made the first field goal. Winning the ball helps, but a missed first shot hands the chance to the other side.
