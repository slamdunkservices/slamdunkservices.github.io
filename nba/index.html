How the picks work

We build ensembles of machine learning models on NBA play-by-play data, player bios, projected starting lineups and game lines. One set of models predicts the jump ball, one predicts which team scores first, one predicts which player scores first for their team, and one predicts how. Combined, they give a probability for every player, market and method in every game. The whole pipeline is written up in How Do You Predict First Baskets?

Every 30 minutes we pull the current prices from the books and convert them to probabilities. When a book’s price is at least 1.5 percentage points better than our projection, it is a play. Unit sizes come from the Kelly criterion, scaled so the average play is about one unit, so a 3-unit recommendation means a bigger edge than a 1-unit one.

When NBA picks post

The first alerts for a slate post in the morning, when books open their first-basket markets. Prices move all day as bettors get involved and injury news lands, and the biggest moves come when starting lineups are confirmed, usually 30 to 60 minutes before tipoff. We keep alerting until the game starts. Our advice on timing is in Is There A Best Time Of Day To Bet First Baskets?

How many NBA picks are there per night?

In 2025-26 we alerted 12,507 plays over 212 game days, about 60 per day across all markets, or roughly ten per game. Most are first-basket plays. You do not need to bet all of them; the unit sizes tell you which edges are largest.

Which sportsbooks do the NBA picks cover?

DraftKings, FanDuel, BetRivers, Hard Rock, bet365, BetMGM, Fanatics, Caesars and theScore. DraftKings and FanDuel carried about two thirds of our 2025-26 volume. Every alert lists each book where the play qualifies, so you can take the best price available to you.
