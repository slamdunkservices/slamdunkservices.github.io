Who are you nerds?

We’re a bunch of data scientists and engineers who use machine learning and artificial intelligence to project who will score first, and how they’ll do it, for every NBA and WNBA regular season and playoff game — plus who’ll go deep and whether the first inning scores for every MLB game. Slam Dunk Bets has been at this since 2020; more on the About page.

How much does it cost?

$19.99 a week, $49.99 a month, or $499.99 a year, and every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. Prices are the same whether you subscribe through Sharpduel, our preferred partner, or Whop. Details on the Subscribe page.

How do I get the picks?

Subscribe through Sharpduel or Whop and you get access to our Discord server, where plays post automatically all day with recommended unit sizes, plus the Slam Dunk Dashboard at app.slamdunk.bet, where you sign in with Discord or Google and see every edge, every book’s price, and the full graded record.

Any notable first basket props you don’t project?

We don’t do first basket of second half yet. And we also don’t project for games in the future (i.e. games for tomorrow). None of this is set in stone, and we do try to keep up with new props as they become available!

Do you track your bets?

We track every bet we recommend and update our ROI totals by market every day on our Discord. Our programmatically tracked betting records go back to the 2021-22 NBA season, and the season-to-date record for every market is always visible in the Dashboard.

What kind of ROI are we talking about then?

We’ve cleared 8,000+ net units across our tracked NBA, WNBA and MLB picks since 2021-22. Unit sizes vary from bettor to bettor, but common units are $100, $10, or $1. Season by season, regular season plus playoffs, with ROI measured on units staked:

  • 2025-26 NBA: +2,135 units (16.4% ROI)
  • 2024-25 NBA: +1,018 units (8.2% ROI)
  • 2023-24 NBA: +1,692 units (11.8% ROI)
  • 2022-23 NBA: +1,610 units*
  • 2026 WNBA: +365 units (11.6% ROI) through August 30
  • 2025 WNBA: +529 units (17.0% ROI)
  • 2024 WNBA: +418 units (21.6% ROI)
  • 2026 MLB, since baseball launched on July 16: home runs +126 units (6.0% ROI) and NRFI/YRFI +3 units (0.7% ROI). Stolen bases and batter props started in September and are too new to read anything into.

These are the standardized-unit totals from our graded ledgers as of September 14, 2026. The live record for every market is in the Dashboard.

* reflects a different application of kelly criterion than we used moving forward, i.e. a smaller kelly factor.

Is this legal where I live?

Slam Dunk Bets is an information service: we publish projections and picks, we don’t take bets or hold anyone’s money. You have to be 21 or older (or the legal betting age where you live), and it’s on you to follow the laws of your state or country when you place a bet. If gambling stops being fun, the National Problem Gambling Helpline is 1-800-GAMBLER.
